Star catalogue: HYG Database
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The star positions, magnitudes, colours and names are derived from the HYG
Database v4.1 by David Nash (astronexus), https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database,
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
licence, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes: only stars brighter than magnitude 4.5 are kept, with a few of the
fields; positions are precessed from J2000 to the date being observed. The
derived catalogue (src/data/stars.json) is shared under the same licence,
CC BY-SA 4.0.
